# Shooting Range (module)

## Overview
- Allow players to tune/calibrate/sight individual firearms, making them worth more.
- Let customers pay to use the shooting range (passive income).
- Improve it by adding more lanes and more training programs.

## Range customers
- Customers pay to enter the shooting range, and leave immediately after the store closes.
- Customers will not enter the store to use the shooting range after 1800.
- Customers generally take 2 hours of shift time before they're finished.
- Some customers will pay again to keep using the lane they're using, rather than leaving at
  the end of their turn.
- No customers will try to use the shooting range if there are no slots available.
- Customers who use the shooting range also have a chance to buy items like anyone else, but
  they always wait until they're done with the shooting range before shopping.

## Unlocking and getting there
- The shooting range is accessible through a placed door.
- To complete the unlock, you must place a set of stairs for the shooting range. The purchase
  is not official until the stairs are placed.
- The stairs go down. They're classic 16-bit style stairs set into the floor, appearing to
  disappear downward.
- Anyone can walk over or stand on the stairs (no collision).
- Pressing E on the stairs takes you down them, just like the backroom door lets you enter the
  backroom, but you MUST be standing on the stairs.
