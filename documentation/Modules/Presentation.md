# Presentation Level (module)

## Overview
Your store space is valuable, but if your store doesn't look good, people may be put off. The
bigger your store, the more people expect, and the more customers you'll have expecting it.

## Decorations
- Holographic displays
- Wall decorations
- Potted plants

Presentation is determined by the number and variety of decorations you have.

## Presentation level
- Maximum: 20, +20 for each store expansion purchased, +2 for each Store Level beyond Store
  Level 5. So the higher your store level, the more decoration you need to meet expectations.

## Customer reactions
- Like cleanliness, each customer has a presentation expectation.
- Customers judge presentation as a percentage. Example: an expectation of 87 means 87%, so
  having 87/100 presentation points or more meets it.
- Low presentation never makes anyone leave, but it completely blocks reputation gain: if your
  presentation is below a customer's expectation, you can still lose reputation from them as
  normal, but you can't gain any, even if you'd otherwise earn it.
