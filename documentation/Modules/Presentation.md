# Presentation Level (module)

## Overview
Your store space is valuable, but if your store doesn't look good, people may be put off. The
bigger your store, the more people expect, and the more customers you'll have expecting it.

## Decorations
The Basic Fixture Supply contract unlocks exactly 20 purchasable designs in `data/Fixtures.json`:
10 holographic displays, 6 freestanding hanging banners, and 4 light fixtures. Their procedural
pixel art is generated by `js/world/DecorationArt.js` before fixtures or placement previews draw.
Banners stand on their own bases; no wall mounting is needed. Each design has a distinct motif,
not just a different color.

Buy them in the Fixtures tab; they arrive in ordinary fixture crates on the Loading Pad.
Place, move, crate up, and sell them through the normal fixture workflow while closed.
Decorations have small footprints and solid feet, leaving their upper artwork passable.
They can also be placed in the backroom, but only decorations on unlocked sales floors contribute.

The store always starts with 20 presentation points, not the current maximum. Every placed
decoration adds its catalog points, and each distinct design adds a once-per-design variety
bonus (1 point by default in `data/tuning/presentation.json`). Duplicate copies add their normal
points but do not repeat the variety bonus. Removing, crating, or moving a decoration into the
backroom removes its contribution immediately; removing the last copy also removes its variety bonus.
Purchase cards show texture previews and explain the contribution.

Prices and presentation points are provisional development defaults, not balance promises.

Crates left on the sales floor lower presentation, giving players a reason to keep spare
stock in the backroom.

### Crates on the sales floor
- Each crate resting on the sales-floor ground costs 1 presentation point. Count every crate
  in a floor stack separately. Do not count crates in the backroom, being carried, or loaded
  in a hovercart.
- Use the cleanliness upset thresholds as presentation expectations: Scavengers 40-60%,
  regular customers 55-75%, Mercenaries 65-80%, and Explorers 70-80%. Falling below a
  customer's expectation blocks reputation gains from them, but never makes them leave or
  causes an extra reputation loss.
- At full starting presentation (20/20), four floor crates leave presentation at 80%; five
  leave it at 75%. That keeps a small amount of stock on the floor from immediately affecting
  customers, while a larger pile can block gains from customers with higher expectations.

Cap the starting points plus decoration contribution at the current maximum **before**
subtracting floor-crate penalties, and never allow the result below zero. Excess decorations
therefore cannot cancel unlimited clutter. Example: at a maximum of 44, 20 starting points
plus 40 decoration points are capped to 44; five floor crates leave 39/44.

## Presentation level
- Maximum: 20, +20 for each store expansion purchased, +2 for each Store Level beyond Store
  Level 5. So the higher your store level, the more decoration you need to meet expectations.

## Customer reactions
- Like cleanliness, each customer has a presentation expectation.
- Customers judge presentation as a percentage. Example: an expectation of 87 means 87%, so
  having 87/100 presentation points or more meets it.
- Low presentation never makes anyone leave, but it completely blocks reputation gain: if your
  presentation is below a customer's expectation, you can still lose reputation from them as
  normal, but you can't gain any, even if you'd otherwise earn it.
