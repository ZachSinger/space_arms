# Customer experience questions

The idea so far: every customer quietly scores their visit from 1 to 5 stars. Those scores add
up to a store rating you can see, great ratings earn rewards, bad ratings may cost you, and
customers leave written reviews under made-up usernames that you can read somewhere in the game.

Write your answer under each question. Skip any you want me to decide, and I'll say what I
picked.

## Reputation

The game already has reputation, which customers raise or lower and which unlocks shop levels.
How should the star rating relate to it? For example, is it a completely separate number, does
it feed into reputation, or should it replace some of what reputation does today?

Your answer: Hmm let's talk about it - it could be a modifier for reputation, where high star ratings boost reputation slightly and low star ratings reduce it. This way, reputation still reflects overall performance, but star ratings give more immediate feedback on individual visits. It's also a way to inject humour, flavor and feedback into the game. 

## What a visit is scored on

Here are things already in the game that a customer could notice during a visit. Which of these
should count, what should be added, and are any of them much more important than the others?

- How long they waited in the checkout line.
- Whether they found everything on their shopping list, or some of it was out of stock.
- How clean the shop was (trash and dirt).
- The shop's presentation (decorations, crates left on the floor).
- Prices compared to what they expected to pay.
- Getting hit by a thrown crate.
- Being crowded or blocked by other customers, staff, or hovercarts.
- Giving up and leaving without buying anything.

Your answer: I think all of these factors should count, but some more than others. For example, long wait times and out-of-stock items might weigh more heavily than minor cleanliness issues. We could also add things like staff friendliness, ease of finding items, and the overall atmosphere of the shop. Each factor could have a different weight in the final star rating.
