## How the hovercart works today

You buy the hovercart at Store Level 7 for 2,200. It holds 3 crates. With no crates on it, it
moves a little slower than you walk. Each crate slows it down more, and with 3 crates it moves
at about 60% of your walking speed. For comparison, you walk at 160 and run at 280. The cart
moves at 150 when empty and 96 with 3 crates.

A full battery holds 300 energy. Moving with 3 crates on it uses 10 energy per second, so a
full charge lasts 30 seconds of moving. Moving empty uses 4 per second, so a full charge lasts
75 seconds. Charging from empty to full on the charger takes 60 seconds.

## Where you buy upgrades

Upgrades are bought in the same Equipment section of the Manager's Tablet Layout tab where you
buy the hovercart, while the store is closed. They take effect right away. There are three
separate upgrade paths: Length and Speed have 3 steps each, and Battery has 6. Each step unlocks
at a Store Level and must be bought in order. Upgrades belong to one hovercart; if you own more
than one, each cart is upgraded separately.

## Length (how many crates it holds)

Each step adds room for one more crate on the cart's stack.

Step 1 unlocks at Level 13, costs 4,000, and raises the cart to 4 crates.

Step 2 unlocks at Level 22, costs 14,000, and raises the cart to 5 crates.

Step 3 unlocks at Level 32, costs 40,000, and raises the cart to 6 crates.

Note: more crates also use more battery. With 6 crates, the cart would use 16 energy per second
while moving.

## Speed (how fast it follows you)

Each step makes the empty cart faster and makes each crate slow it down less. The last step
gets it to your full running speed no matter how many crates are on it, which is the goal from
your original hovercart notes.

Step 1 unlocks at Level 10 and costs 3,000. Empty, it moves at 190. Each crate slows it by 12.

Step 2 unlocks at Level 20 and costs 12,000. Empty, it moves at 235. Each crate slows it by 6.

Step 3 unlocks at Level 37 and costs 45,000. It moves at 280, your running speed, with any
number of crates.

## Battery (how long it runs)

Each step makes the battery bigger. Charging from empty to full still takes 60 seconds at every
step, so a bigger battery never means a longer wait at the charger.

Step 1 unlocks at Level 13, costs 3,000, and gives 450 energy. That's 45 seconds of moving with
3 crates.

Step 2 unlocks at Level 22, costs 8,000, and gives 600 energy. That's 60 seconds of moving with
3 crates.

Step 3 unlocks at Level 32, costs 15,000, and gives 800 energy. That's 50 seconds of moving with
6 crates.

Step 4 unlocks at Level 37, costs 30,000, and gives 1,000 energy. That's about 62 seconds of
moving with 6 crates.

Step 5 unlocks at Level 42, costs 60,000, and gives 1,300 energy. That's about 81 seconds of
moving with 6 crates.

Step 6 unlocks at Level 49, costs 100,000, and gives 1,600 energy. That's 100 seconds of moving
with 6 crates, more than three times the starting cart with 3 crates.

## Why these levels and prices

The prices follow the store's own big purchases: the sales floor expansions cost 6,400 at Level
13, 53,000 at Level 32, and 135,500 at Level 42. Each upgrade costs a fraction of the expansion
around the same level, so upgrades feel worth saving for without competing with expanding the
store. Speed comes first, at Level 10, because a slow cart is the first thing you'll notice.
Length and Battery arrive with the bigger store at Level 13, when trips get longer.
