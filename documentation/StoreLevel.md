## Store Level

These are starting values for playtesting, not final balance. The reputation shown is
the total store reputation needed at the end of a shift, not an amount spent. An earned
level stays unlocked if reputation later falls.

All rewards listed for Levels 2-30 are implemented. Supplier contracts unlock at their listed
Shop Levels; faction reputation requirements still apply where specified. Sales-floor and
backroom expansions become available to buy at their listed levels, cost credits, and must
be bought in order while the store is closed. Paid contracts and hiring staff still cost
credits. Every employee can be hired from the start of staffing at the same price; staff-limit
levels only raise how many can be hired at once. Existing signed contracts and hired
employees remain available.

The hovercart (and its upgrades at Levels 3, 8, 14, 21, and 28) and the Expanded, Premium
Shelf, Bulk Display, and Armory Display fixture licenses were removed. Older saves are
refunded what they paid for them, and any stock on them goes to the Go-Backs bin. Level 8 currently has no reward.
