## Store Level

These are starting values for playtesting, not final balance. The reputation shown is
the total store reputation needed at the end of a shift, not an amount spent. An earned
level stays unlocked if reputation later falls.

Levels 2-4 have the first specific unlocks in the game. The Level 5-30 unlocks below
are planned, not implemented yet; level numbers and reputation thresholds work through
Level 30. Other existing supplier contracts still use their current reputation
requirements until assigned to a level. Signing a contract and hiring staff still cost
credits. Existing signed contracts and hired employees remain available.
