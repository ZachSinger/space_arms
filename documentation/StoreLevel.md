## Store Level

These are starting values for playtesting, not final balance. The reputation shown is
the total store reputation needed at the end of a shift, not an amount spent. An earned
level stays unlocked if reputation later falls.

All rewards from Levels 2-30 are implemented. Sales-floor and backroom expansions become
available to buy at their listed levels, cost credits, and must be bought in order while
the store is closed. Other supplier contracts still use their reputation requirements
until assigned to a level. Signing a contract and hiring staff still cost credits.
Every employee can be hired from the start of staffing at the same price; staff-limit levels
only raise how many can be hired at once. Existing signed contracts, hired employees, and
hovercart upgrades remain available.
Hovercart upgrades are bought in order in the Equipment tab.
