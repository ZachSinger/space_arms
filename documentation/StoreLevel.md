## Store Level

These are starting values for playtesting, not final balance. The reputation shown is
the total store reputation needed at the end of a shift, not an amount spent. An earned
level stays unlocked if reputation later falls.

The current curve has 50 levels. Serving every arriving customer without reputation
penalties earns one level after each of the first four shifts, reaching Level 5. Later
levels take several clean shifts as the reputation gaps grow. This assumes customers
can find what they want and complete their purchases; it is not a shift-completion bonus.
The 50-level count and later gaps are first-pass tuning choices, not a fixed run-length target.

Every level from 2 to 50 unlocks one or two things. Supplier contracts unlock at their listed
Shop Levels; faction reputation requirements still apply where specified. Sales-floor and
backroom expansions become available to buy at their listed levels, cost credits, and must
be bought in order while the store is closed. Paid contracts and hiring staff still cost
credits. Every employee can be hired from the start of staffing at the same price; staff-limit
levels only raise how many can be hired at once.

Contract and expansion prices grow with what a shop at that level earns: a contract costs a
little under half of what the shop makes while working through that level, and an expansion
costs about a full level's earnings. Hiring costs 500 and each employee's base wage is 100
per shift. Existing signed contracts, bought expansions,
and hired employees remain available in older saves, even where their unlock level moved later.

How the unlocks are ordered:
- Levels 1-5 offer at most 15 products in total, so the first shifts stay simple.
- A gun arrives together with, or after, the ammo it fires. Ammo-only contracts arrive with a gun
  that uses them (for example, Blackwood Autocannon Feed comes with the Vanguard Rotary Gun).
- A brand's attachments arrive with or after that brand's guns.
- Contracts that need group reputation keep their original order, so the group requirements
  still rise steadily.
- Staff limits stay at Levels 4, 7, 10, 15, 18, and 23, because customer numbers grow quickly
  early and level off after about Level 15; extra staff later would arrive after they're needed.
- Each expansion arrives once there's enough new stock to fill it.

Ammo and firearms now unlock together: the scatter-rifle arrives with scatter shells at Level 16,
the RPG arrives with rockets at Level 38, and a separate Pre-War Ammunition contract unlocks
heavy pistol and rifle ammunition alongside the first matching firearms at Level 41.

Planned modules that will need an unlock level: Shooting Range and Presentation. Drone Deliveries
are available from the start. The Hovercart unlocks at Level 7.
